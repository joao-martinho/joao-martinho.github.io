Projects

My most significant personal project is ORBE, a web application based on Spring Boot and plain HTML/JavaScript that I wrote as my undergraduate thesis in 2025. Its purpose was to help professors and students in the CS department handle the document exchange required for the thesis preparation process.

Originally, the name Orbe was not meant as an acronym, but a word referring to a sphere or globe, and in the context of my project it had no actual meaning; it was just a name I found pleasant-sounding and simple. Later, I decided to adopt it retroactively as a recursive acronym: ORBE Registra Boletins Eletrônicos (in English: ORBE Records Electronic Bulletins).

Additionally, I wrote EPUB Cleaner, a Shell script to normalize and standardize EPUB files according to a custom style sheet.

Recommended reading

  • End of 10 - everything you need to know about the end of Windows 10 support and the reasons to migrate your computer to Linux.
  • Keep Android Open - a protest initiative against Google's requirement to register all developers, which poses a threat to independent app stores and an open Android.
  • Use plain text email - an article about the upsides of using plain text over HTML for emails. Includes email client recommendations and step-by-step guides to set them up.
